# V4.3 point-in-time breadth ablation — 2026-07-31

## Decision

V4.3 breadth is a promising research candidate, but it is not eligible to replace the current
shadow or production models. Both horizons improved their point estimates, yet neither satisfied
all predeclared statistical gates. No model was promoted and existing shadow jobs were untouched.

## Audited data contract

- Coverage interval: 2020-01-28 through 2026-05-28 (1,592 US sessions).
- Mean / P05 / minimum / latest member coverage: 96.60% / 95.25% / 95.05% / 97.42%.
- Ambiguous provider identities: 36 explicitly quarantined symbols.
- Dataset: 59,356 rows, 19 assets, 92 columns, 30- and 90-session targets.
- Breadth fields: ten model features plus three non-model diagnostic fields.
- Integrity: zero duplicate asset/cutoff/horizon keys, zero missing or non-finite breadth values.
- Dataset provenance: `data/equity_sp500_breadth_v43.metadata.json`.

The coverage start was selected solely from coverage and identity quality before inspecting model
outcomes. The original 2013-present gate remains closed.

## Regime observations

Against the base ordinal model, 30-session breadth improved Brier in bull, sideways, broad, mixed,
narrow, high-dispersion, normal-dispersion and low-dispersion segments. It worsened the historical
bear segment by 0.00151 Brier. At 90 sessions it improved every reported regime segment; the
largest gains appeared in low dispersion (-0.00480), bear (-0.00485), sideways (-0.00434) and
narrow breadth (-0.00382). These are diagnostic subgroup results, not separate promotion claims.

## Why the gate did not open

- The required BSS is at least 5%; breadth reached 1.42% at 30 sessions and 1.78% at 90 sessions.
- Both paired confidence intervals versus the base model still include zero.
- The 90-session breadth ECE is 0.0507 historically and 0.0571 in 2026, above the 5% target.
- Nested, complexity-penalized selection did not consistently choose the broader models.
- There is no new forward shadow evidence for this exact breadth feature contract.

## Next controlled experiment

Freeze the present reports and avoid selecting more feature subsets on the already-inspected
2023–2026 outcomes. The next candidate should be predeclared before evaluation: a compact,
economically distinct breadth subset or stronger calibration regularization, followed by new
walk-forward periods and forward shadow scoring. V4.3 should only enter a new shadow lane after the
daily full-index breadth inputs can be produced under the same identity and availability contract.
